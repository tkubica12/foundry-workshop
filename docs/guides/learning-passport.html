Release rule

Release requires evidence about your own fully fingerprinted candidate: all 8 development and 4 holdout cases attempted, zero incomplete, timed-out, or access-blocked cases, at least 7/8 and 3/4 passes, every critical assertion passing, no critical regression, the relevant operational budget passing, and separate T02/T03 authorization proof. This worksheet checks those entries for consistency; it does not validate the underlying cloud evidence.
